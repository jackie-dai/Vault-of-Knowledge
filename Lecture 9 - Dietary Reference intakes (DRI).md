

## Nutrient reference value (NRV)
estimates nutrient requirements and are useful benchmarks for assessing nutritional needs

First set of format NRVs (Recommend Dietary Allowances RDA) were established in **1941**
	- formal values from 1941-
	- replaced by Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI) 

Requested by the National Defense advisory commission due to the malunion caused by war and economic depression
