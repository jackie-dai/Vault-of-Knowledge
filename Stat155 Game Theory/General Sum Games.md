
## Nash Equilibrium
A pair of mixed strategies that each player won't deviate from unilaterally 

$$
(x^*)^TAy^* \ge x^TAy^* 
$$
*for all x*
The expected payoff for player 1 is playing this mixed strategy is better or equal to all payoffs when player 2 is playing their best response.

$$
(x^*)^TBy^* \ge (x^*)^TBy 
$$

**Mixed Strategy**
list of probabilities x1, x2, ..., that player 1 will play action_i 

**Pure Strategy**
A mixed strategy that play plays one action with probability 1

## Pareto Optimality
There is no other outcome where you cannot make a player better off without making at least one other player worse off.

Pareto improvement: moving from one outcome to another that benefits all players
Pareto optimal: An outcome where no pareto improvements can be made.

*The nash equilibrium is not always pareto optimal*
