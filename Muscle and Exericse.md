
ATP: Adenosine triphosphate. A molecule that cells use to supply energy immediately. Muscles store very little ATP and needs to quickly regenerate it (from carbs and fat).

Creatine replenishes ATP more rapidly by adding phosphocreatine. This allows you to strength train harder and get that extra rep in. It does not mean direct muscle growth or increase in oxygen supply for endurance training

Anerobic: strength training, brief exercises that uses muscle energy/ATP
Aerobic: endurance training, prolonged exercises that relies on your oxygen supply
