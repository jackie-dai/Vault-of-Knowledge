

When estimates $\theta$ are unbiased, their mean squared error MSE is equal to the variance (or equivalently standard error)

If the true value is $\theta=10$, and the estimator averages out to 10
Then the bias $\theta-\hat{\theta} = 0$ and therefore, the MSE = Var(X) + Bias(X)^2 => MSE=Var(X)

*Variance is important when it comes to unbiased estimators*
