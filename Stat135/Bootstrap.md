
## Types of Bootstraps
### Parametric Bootstrap
Assume the distribution of the population, sample from the estimated distribution, and keep resampling

### Nonparametric Bootstrap
Resample from the sample. Only assumptions being independence and large sample size

## Procedure
1. Took a sample of the population
2. Resample the same sample b times w/o replacement
	1. Each sample, compute the observed bootstrap estimate
3. Estimate quantities (mean, variance) from the bootstrap distribution

*A important note is that the bootstrap distribution is not centered at the true population parameter, it is centered at the observed statistic, giving us the accuracy of the original sample*
