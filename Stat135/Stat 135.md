


## Sampling Distribution
![[Pasted image 20261014002718.png]]
Each time we take a sample from a distribution and plug into our estimator, we get a different estimate; this is based on the data from the random sample. 
The estimator has a sampling distribution and tells us what to expect from a estimate.


Datasets: [https://stat4ds.rwth-aachen.de/data/](https://stat4ds.rwth-aachen.de/data/)
