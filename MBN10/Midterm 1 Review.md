## Lecture 3 - Body Fat and Obesity
Fat is stored as triglycerides (TAGS=3 fatty acids) inside fat cells called adipocytes

Obesity brings several risks
- CVD -> high blood pressure

Locations of fat in the human body
Visceral fat - inside the belly, around the intestines, and liver

having visceral fat means worse lipids and higher blood pressure -> resulting in far more diabetes

Subcutaneous fat is on the outside and not as dangerous as visceral fat

Hypertrophic (fewer bigger cells) are the bad cells, hyperplastic (many small cells) is much safer

Big and visceral cells release fatty acids into the blood, while small subcataneous cells hold onto theirs
