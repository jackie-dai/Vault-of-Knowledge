
### Unsaturated vs saturated fat
The difference is not in the calories per gram, but the chemical structure. 

Saturated fat: no carbon-carbon double bonds
Unsaturated: one or multiple double bonds


Lipoproteins act as the vehicle for transporting 
Triglyceride/cholesterol is the cargo being transported


## Dietary Cholesterol
The body needs cholesterol, but too much and it can reduce absorption efficiency. 
A foods cholesterol content does not translate directly into your bodys blood cholesterol concentration
