
The most difficult part of weight loss is maintaining it. When the body loses weight, it
- reduces energy expenditure (b/c there's less mass to move around)
- Increases appetite 

## GLP-1
A hormone that affects eating. Medicines acting on this system can be used to reduce food intake and body weight. 

What it does
- increase insulin secretion (regulates glucose levels)
- moves food slower through digestion system (feel full for longer)
- reduces appetite
