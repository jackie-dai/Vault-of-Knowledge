
**Insulin**: a hormone that converts sugar into energy for your body

So a higher insulin resistance means your body struggles to turn sugar into energy efficiently.

But it doesn't necessarily mean people with insulin resistance will get diabetes, the body can adapt. For example, the beta cells in the pancreas will secrete more insulin to meet the demand of insulin.

Glucose: a sugar that travels through your bloodstream

Insulin sensitivity means to require less insulin, reducing the workload of the beta cell function.

What leads to severe type 2 diabetes is when the beta cell function declines and can meet the insulin demand.

### Pregnancy
Increase insulin resistance, putting the pancreas through a stress test to produce enough insulin
