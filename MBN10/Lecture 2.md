
## Why care about Obesity?

Being overweight and unfit is the leading cause of colon cancer

## Is all body fat the same?
Is all the fat the same? Is there worse fat and better fat?

Depends on
1. Fat location
2. Cell size and number
	1. big fat cells are worse to have than small fat cells. This is because big fat cells and visceral fat cells tend to release more fatty acids (are more lipolytic)
	
"Apple shape" -> abdominal fat
"Pear shape" -> butt and thigh fat

Fat around your liver and organs is much worse than exterior fat

*Exercising gets rid of the visceral fat cells first*

People who are overweight but fit (in terms of treadmill testing) are at a lower risk of cardiovascular disease (CVD) than those who do not exercise.
